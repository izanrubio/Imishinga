Design system

Accent: indigo. It reads as focused, stays clear of the red/amber/green status hues, and holds AA contrast as white-on-fill and as ink-on-soft. Stone neutrals add the warmth.
Font: Instrument Sans. Slightly condensed, friendly letterforms that stay legible at 12px in dense cards; system sans is the fallback.
